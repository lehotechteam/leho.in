# leho.in

**⚠️ LEGACY WEBSITE - This is an old, static HTML website maintained for archival purposes.**

Official website of LEHO (Ladakh Environment and Health Organization), a non-profit organization established on July 1, 1991, working on sustainable development, ecology, and health issues in Ladakh, India.

## 🏛️ About LEHO

Ladakh Environment and Health Organisation (LEHO) was founded to address issues of sustainable development, ecology, and health in response to changes in agriculture, food habits, social values, and culture of different Ladakhi societies. The organization promotes a holistic approach to natural resource utilization integrated with traditional Ladakhi culture.
